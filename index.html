MATERI PEMBELAJARAN

Descriptive Text

  • 1. Defenition

    Descriptive Text is a text that describes a particular person, place, or thing in detail. (Teks yang menggambarkan orang, tempat, atau benda tertentu secara rinci).

  • 2. Social Function (Fungsi Sosial)

    To describe and reveal a particular person, place, or thing. (Untuk mendeskripsikan dan mengenalkan orang, tempat, atau benda tertentu).

  • 3. Generic Structure (Struktur Teks)

    ⁕ Identification: Mengenalkan objek/orang/tempat yang akan dideskripsikan (terletak di paragraf pertama).

    ⁕ Description: Menggambarkan ciri-ciri khusus, sifat, penampilan, atau bagian-bagian dari objek tersebut (paragraf selanjutnya).

  • 4. Language Features (Unsur Kebahasaan)

    ⁕ Focus on Specific Participant: Menggambarkan objek yang spesifik (contoh: Borobudur Temple, My Cat, Mr. John — bukan hal umum seperti temples atau cats).

    ⁕ Use of Simple Present Tense: Menggunakan kata kerja bentuk pertama karena menyatakan fakta (contoh: It is located in..., She has long hair).

    ⁕ Use of Adjectives (Kata Sifat): Kata yang menjelaskan ciri-ciri (contoh: beautiful, ancient, tall, friendly, white).

  • 5. Contoh Descriptive Text

    Borobudur Temple

    (Paragraf 1 - Identification) Borobudur Temple is one of the most famous Buddhist temples in the world. It is located in Magelang, Central Java, Indonesia.

    (Paragraf 2 - Description) Borobudur is a magnificent stone structure. It consists of nine stacked platforms, six square and three circular, topped by a central dome. The temple is decorated with thousands of relief panels and many Buddha statues. Every year, millions of tourists visit this breathtaking historical place.
